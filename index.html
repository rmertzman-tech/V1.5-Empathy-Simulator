🔄 From PRF Isolation to Coordination: OS1 → OS2

Most people are trapped in PRF isolation: They can only work with people who share their moral foundations. When someone with Care 4.5 meets someone with Fairness 4.5, they argue endlessly because their warrants (reasons why things matter) are incommensurable.

Operating System 1 (OS1): Coordination through Belief/Reason/Observation/Agreement. Requires shared warrants. Works within ~10-15% of humanity (people very similar to you).

Operating System 2 (OS2): Coordination through capability complementarity. Doesn't require warrant agreement. Instead, you identify what each person's PRF enables them to do well, find a shared task, and pool capabilities. Works with ~80-90% of humanity.

This app builds the foundational skill: You can't shift to OS2 if you can't read PRFs accurately. Master individual PRF reading here, then advance to OS2 Coordination Training (next module).

As you practice with these 20 vignettes, ask yourself: "What could this person contribute that someone with the opposite PRF pattern couldn't? How might they complement each other?"
